Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about EstateX.

How do I search for property listings?

You can search for property listings by visiting the "Properties" page. From there, you can filter by location, property type, price range, and number of bedrooms to find exactly what you're looking for.

How can I book a viewing?

To book a viewing, you must first create a Customer account and sign in. Then, navigate to the specific property you are interested in and click the "Book Viewing" button. Select your preferred date and time, and the agent will confirm your appointment.

How do I contact a property agent directly?

Each property listing displays the details of the certified agent managing it. If you are signed in, you can view their direct contact information and initiate a booking or inquiry from the property page.
